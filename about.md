I worked as a software developer for four years before realizing I wanted to shift into the ever-growing, ever-more-relevant field of penetration testing. I find it combines my natural aptitude in writing and my ability to talk with people with the more technical stuff that I love to research and play with. 

### about the blog

I write these blog posts primarily to help my own understanding of things I’ve encountered in my studies of penetration testing. A lot of this information is gleaned from my own research of official documentation and other informative blogs and tutorials. If I heavily reference a specific source I will cite it directly with a footnote, and I also try to link other resources that helped inform me on the topic at hand. This blog is closer akin to me taking notes on specific subjects, versus thorough tutorials (though I hope to write those someday as well). Please email me if you have any problems or corrections with anything I state here, and recall that I’m in the process of learning about this stuff so I’m very open to hearing from those with more experience in the field. Thanks for reading.
